Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

TripM8 AI Inc. (“TripM8”, “we”, “us”) builds AI agents for travel businesses and travelers. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and services, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us directly, such as your name, email address, and company when you contact us or join a waitlist.
  • Travel and booking details you or your business share with our AI agents in order to deliver the service.
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, collected automatically when you browse this site.

How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and improve our products and the TripM8 agent network.
  • To respond to enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To keep our services secure and to meet our legal obligations.

Sharing

We share information with travel suppliers and partners only as needed to fulfil a booking or request you have made, and with service providers who process data on our behalf under contract. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, and we use technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@tripm8.ai.
